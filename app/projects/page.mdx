# Projects

## [Fantasy Survivor](https://fantasy-tv-eosin.vercel.app/)

A free fantasy league for the TV show Survivor. Players draft 3 of the 21 castaways and score points each episode from what their picks do: challenge wins, idols, votes, and surviving another week. Each week they also guess who goes home. Scores go on a global leaderboard and on private leaderboards for groups of friends.
