# Analysis and Improvements to Monopoly Deal

As a stocking stuffer in Christmas 2009, I received a Monopoly Deal. As a Junior in high school, my friend played this game religiously. We would play it whenever we met up in college. Then 10 years pass and somehow in the 2020s this game is making a resurgence. After a decade of playing this game, I have to say that it is not very good... BUT here is how you fix it.

## TLDR:

### Rules Updates

1. JSNs can only be played on 1. Action cards targeting the player holding the Just say No and 2. Pass Go's.
2. All rent cards can be played as any other rent card for 1/2 the price.
3. Double the rent does not take up an action slot.
4. A full set of the railroads consisting of no wild cards counts as 2 full property sets. Upon completion, everyone else must golf clap and compliment as if they are the Monopoly guy. 
5. House and Hotel can be played on to full property sets from a player's bank at the cost of an action slot per card.
6. Make one of the 1M be a "steal your entire bank"
7. Make 1-2 of the 1M be "steal a DB"
8. All remaining 1M are pass gos
9. Pass gos are scry: draw 2.
10. After the deck has been reshuffled, renting into the negative eliminates that player.
11. If you rent a player with a property wild card into the negative, you get the property wild card.
12. The game ends immediately after someone achieves 3 full sets.

And now the game is good. You're welcome. Hit me up if you try it and have thoughts.
