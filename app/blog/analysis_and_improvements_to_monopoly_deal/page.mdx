# Improvements to Monopoly Deal

As a stocking stuffer in Christmas 2009, I received a Monopoly Deal. As a Junior in high school, my friend played this game religiously. We would play it whenever we met up in college. Then 10 years pass and somehow in the 2020s this game is making a resurgence. After a decade of playing this game, I have to say that it is not very good... BUT here is how you fix it.

## TLDR

### Rules Updates

1. JSNs can only be played on Action cards targeting the player with the JSN, or on Pass Go's.
2. All rent cards can be played as any other rent card for 1/2 the price.
3. Double the Rent does not take up a play slot.
4. A full set of railroads with no wild cards counts as 2 full property sets. Upon completion, everyone else must golf clap and provide a compliment as if they are the Monopoly guy.
5. All properties on a player's board must be grouped by color i.e. you cannot split same-color properties across multiple chains (e.g., 3 green properties must all be in one chain, not 2+1). Only all-color wildcards are exempt from this rule (e.g., you can have 2 green properties in a chain with 1 all-color wildcard standing alone on the side).
6. When a color wildcard is played or gained, it must declare its color. A color wildcard can be unlinked for one play slot to switch to its opposite color.
7. When an all-color wildcard is played or gained, it must be declared whether it is standalone or part of a property chain. After being established, an all-color wildcard can be moved to join or leave a property chain for one play slot.
8. If you play or gain a card matching the color of a completed full set making a wildcard in that chain redundant, you may reposition the wildcard for free.
9. Houses and Hotels can be played onto full property sets from a player's bank at the cost of one play slot per card.
10. One of the 1M cards is a "Steal Bank" card.
11. One or two of the 1M cards are "Steal Card" cards.
12. All remaining 1M cards are Pass Go's (draw 2).
13. After the entire deck has been drawn through and reshuffled, renting a player into the negative eliminates them.
14. If you rent a player with an all-color wildcard into the negative at any time, you claim the all-color wildcard.
15. The game ends immediately after someone achieves 3 full sets.

And now the game is good. You're welcome. Hit me up if you try it and have thoughts.
