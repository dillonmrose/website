# The Identity Crisis in Agentic Development

Raise your hand if your agent runs under your identity at work. Keep your hand up if your work identity has access to information that should not escape your company or can make changes that pose a risk to your company or others. Keep your hand up if you are always in auto mode. Join the vast majority of the industry.

<img src="/identity-crisis-meme.gif" className="w-[80%] md:w-[50%] block mx-auto" />

Agent identities are the way out. Your company already has  and I don't see companies pursuing it fast enough. Companies that are adopting the practice of provisioning agent identities seem to have the scale wrong. We don't need 3-4 agent identities per company or organization. We need 100 per developer — as many as they ask for. If your company is prepared for it, scoping the blast radius of an agent at the identity level is far easier than writing PreToolUse hooks. There are agents finding zero-days to break out of sandboxes, and you think your PreToolUse hook blocking rm is effective. I love that for you.
